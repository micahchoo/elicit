## How to read this

| mark | meaning |
|---|---|
| **KEEP** | enters the vault as a Snippet |
| **drop** | stays out — reason code below |
| **⚠ Q-51** | **not your prose.** Cannot enter under Q-1/Q-51 regardless of what you decide |

Reason codes for drops:

- `world` — true regardless of who wrote it. Someone else could have written the
  sentence and it would carry the same information. The Pune case count, the
  potters' courtyard, Janastu's two decades.
- `log` — an activity record with no stance. What you did, in order, with
  nothing said about why or what it meant.
- `scaffold` — talk about the document rather than about the world. "I present
  here…", "Another decision was to structure…".
- `frag` — not a standalone proposition. Needs the sentence before it to mean
  anything. 044's admissibility gate should have caught these and did not.
- `spec` — product description. True of the software, not of you.
- `theirs` — an idea explicitly attributed to someone else in your own words.
  The wording is yours; the belief is not.

**Totals: 139 KEEP, 149 drop, 7 excluded as not your prose.**

The criterion throughout: *would this sentence be evidence about you if you did
not know who wrote it?* Where I was unsure I dropped, because a wrong drop is
recoverable — the source files are not going anywhere — and a wrong keep
becomes a Claim about a person who never held it.

by volume — 76 of 139 — but it no longer contributes a hundred sentences about a
pottery workshop to a wiki that is supposed to be about you.

reaches `evidenced` without corroboration from one of the other eighteen. That
is the vocabulary working. It also means the eighteen small posts carry more
weight than their cut counts suggest: they are the only cross-sitting evidence
the corpus has.
