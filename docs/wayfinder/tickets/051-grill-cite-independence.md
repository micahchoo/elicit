## Question

From the 2026-08-02 HANDOFF review. Must land BEFORE the Clerk writes its
first claim (ticket 008).

The Status vocabulary promotes a claim to `evidenced` at two or more
independent cites. Nothing defines independent. If it means two distinct
snippet ids, then two cuts from one answer on one thread — one thought said
twice — promote a claim, every claim reaches `evidenced` within a month, and
the Status vocabulary stops carrying information. That is the specific way
epistemic metadata usually dies.

The review's proposal, which reads right: independence requires separation
across SITTINGS, and ideally across question source or Facet. A claim that
survives being approached from a different angle is the thing the status is
supposed to mean.

Grill: the exact predicate; whether it is one rule or a ladder (cross-sitting
= evidenced, cross-facet = something stronger); and what it does to claims
minted from a single long sitting, which is what the corpus mostly is today.
