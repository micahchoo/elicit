## Question

From Micah 2026-08-02, offering his "Mr. KTG" curriculum-maker prompt
(domain → skills → know-what / know-how / know-why, with "genres" as
distinct styles of doing and "habits of thinking" under know-how) and
asking whether it is useful for the Coach.

Ruling from the discussion: useful INVERTED. As a curriculum it teaches
downward from the model's map of the domain — a knowledge tree shown to
the person, or their claims graded against it, is the deficit-shaped move
Q-15 forbids, and a checklist against the tree re-smuggles the completion
rates Q-75 banned. The system never measures the person against external
canon.

As the Coach's INTERNAL partition of the question space it fits the
approved plan (docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-02-coach-slice.md) in three
places:

1. **Quest alternatives (Q-74):** the 2-3 alternative acts get a
   structural axis — one act per K-branch (vocabulary/terminology act,
   procedure/practice act, know-why probe). Same house pattern as Q-38's
   "distinct organizing principles, never shuffles of one".
2. **Know-why as elicitation, not teaching:** the layer "nobody speaks
   about" is what Elicit draws out. The decomposition steers COMPOSED
   QUESTIONS toward the person's own philosophies/theories of their
   coached domain — landing as their verbatim words under the existing
   `value` / `causal-theory` / `construct` facets, citable as
   skill-claims. Teaching down produces model prose; asking up produces
   evidence.
3. **Genres are offer-shaped:** "try the <genre> way" is a quest option;
   the return (what fit, what chafed) is know-why through ordinary
   capture (Q-75).

Build: amend the Coach compose prompts only — the quest-offer builder and
the advice/reflection question builders (plan tasks around
`buildAdviceInput` / the offer composition). The decomposition is
scaffolding the model thinks WITH, regenerated per offer; never stored as
an object, never displayed as a tree, never a coverage measure. The
person sees only its shadow: well-differentiated quest options and
questions that reach the unspoken layer.

Hard lines (from canon, restated for the executor): no surface renders
the tree; no advice names a gap against model canon; nothing counts
coverage of branches; prompt-only change — no new types, storage, or
routes.

Sequencing: prompt amendments to modules 090 creates — blocked by 090's
execution, then a small prompt-and-tests task.

Source prompt kept with the ticket for the exemplars (soccer genres,
playmaker habit-of-thinking, Pfender's designer-judgment passage — the
last being the same epistemology as Q-15: judgment terminates with the
person, not the criteria).
