## Question

From the 2026-08-02 HANDOFF review.

Diachronic ("you changed") and synchronic ("you hold both") are the clean
cases and both have machinery. The review argues the common case is neither:
a person who is right in both domains and has not drawn the boundary. Naming
that boundary is plausibly the highest-value thing the system can produce,
and the design currently gives it one sentence as "the missing work" — it
falls out of the Contradiction pipeline as a residual.

Grill: a question form that takes two poles and asks for the DISCRIMINATING
CONDITION — where does the first hold, where does the second, what tells them
apart. Whether it is a QuestionForm, a Protocol instrument, or a third
Contradiction type; what it cites (both poles, Q-12-composed); and what the
answer becomes — a third claim that supersedes neither pole, most likely,
which is a shape the op contract (Q-29) does not have a verb for yet.
