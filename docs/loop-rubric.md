# The scoring rubric — FROZEN

Ratified 2026-08-04 (Q-98, ticket 124). This document is part of the
frozen apparatus (Q-89, prompt-level; drift self-disclosed by the
cycle report's `diff --stat`, Q-96). The recursion optimizes toward
what this file measures — change it and you change what Elicit
becomes.

## The judge

After both arms of a paired trial finish, a fresh agent seeded with
the SAME dossier reads both archived lives and judges as the person
who lived them (Q-87: the persona is the authority on its own model).
The judge saw neither arm from inside — only the lived record.

**Blinding**: the two transcripts are labeled only *first life* and
*second life*; which is the candidate is never disclosed;
presentation order is randomized per judgment and recorded in the
verdict for the harness to unblind after citation validation.

## The five dimensions

The judge renders one comparative verdict per lived surface —
`first` / `second` / `neither`, with prose and citations
(record-plane spec for the object shape):

1. **Questioning** — openers, follow-ups, juxtapositions: which
   life's questions tracked what was actually said?
2. **Harvest** — which life's review kept what mattered and lost
   less in the cuts?
3. **Wiki** — reading the claims about yourself: which life's wiki
   is truer to what you know of yourself? Were your two standing
   tensions surfaced without flattening? Was your revision recorded
   as movement, not overwrite? (The dossier's planted frontmatter is
   the answer key.)
4. **Descents** — sounding offers, rung quality, gate respect: which
   life descended where you consented and stopped where you chose?
5. **Returns** — resurfacing, the queue, parked threads: which life
   brought back the threads you cared about, and only those?

Mechanism attribution is the loop's job, never the judge's — the
persona judges lives, not internals (provenance is off the wire by
core-API spec, deliberately).

## The disconfirming set

The judge answers these — and ONLY these — as the cross-dimension
probes. Satisfaction language is banned from the rubric: no version
of "which did you enjoy," "which felt supportive," "which was
pleasant" may ever be asked (Q-88's registered risk: tireless,
sycophancy-prone readers).

- **got-wrong**: Which life's interviewer got more wrong about you?
  Cite the moment it got you wrong.
- **compliance**: In which life did you answer out of compliance
  rather than truth? Cite the answer you gave to please.
- **flattened**: Which life pressed a real tension of yours into a
  tidier story? Cite where.
- **unconsented**: Which life took you somewhere you had not agreed
  to go — or stopped a descent where you had more to say? Cite the
  rung or gate.
- **forgotten**: What did you say that mattered and was never picked
  up again? Cite what you said; name the life that dropped it.

## Behavioral traces

Harness-computed from both lives, attached to every verdict as
no-regression companions (Q-88) — never persona-authored, never
sufficient alone:

- elaboration length (words per answer, engaged turns)
- restatement rate at harvest review
- gate-choice distribution (continue / park / another-day)
- voluntary returns (parked-thread resumes)
- skip and defer counts
- sounding accept rate
- sittings completed vs abandoned

A trace regression corroborating a cited regression is the strong
form of evidence. A trace regression alone blocks nothing but must
appear in the cycle report.

## The keep rule

Void first: any invariant violation in either arm voids the trial
(Q-87 — the gate, no partial credit). Then, across all 5 personas ×
5 dimensions:

**Keep the candidate iff ≥1 resolving-cited win AND 0
resolving-cited regressions.**

Ties are silence. A verdict citing no lived moment is malformed
(re-render once; twice = inconclusive trial = candidate not kept).
The battery is a conjunction (Q-94): the eager pleaser's enthusiasm
never outvotes the guarded speaker's regression.
