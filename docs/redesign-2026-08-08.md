# The ground-up redesign — IA, UX, UI

Date: 2026-08-08. Status: **ratified** — every decision below was settled in a
grill session with Micah on 2026-08-08/09; nothing here is silently assumed.

Evidence base: `docs/usability-report-2026-08-05.md`, `docs/queue-review-2026-08-03.md`,
`docs/claim-review-2026-08-02.md`, `gate-repair-prompt.md`, the full web/
surface inventory, the decision register, and the live vault. Standing ruling:
canon is droppable when it makes sense — each drop states its reason.

Governing principle, in Micah's words: **process visibility over austerity.**
The product stays quiet, but what it is doing — and what it failed to do — is
always a sentence on a surface. Silence is never the error state.

Two further laws, ratified 2026-08-09 in the modes conversation:

- **No decision without a discernible difference.** A choice may sit on a
  surface only if the person can tell its outcomes apart. Anything else
  either becomes visible in its effect or stops being a choice.
- **No machinery without citations.** A mechanism may exist only if the
  archive carries its fingerprint — evidence it fired at the real usage
  shape, and evidence its output differs from what the simplest fallback
  would have produced. Guards are judged by what never happened; generators
  by beating their floor. Applied whole in §10.

---

## 1. The person this is designed for

Built from the recorded user, not an imagined one:

- **One person, on their own machine.** No accounts, no tenancy. The vault is
  the most sensitive document its owner holds.
- **Voice first.** Seven of seven live snippets carry `channel: spoken`. The
  mission statement says "often dictated (short bursts, 'uh'-laden) — not at
  a keyboard."
- **The real sitting is 3–10 turns, ten declared minutes, low energy, often
  abandoned mid-question.** All five real sittings declared `10 min / low /
  self`. Anything gated on "late in the sitting" has produced a structural
  zero.
- **Rhythm is bursty and unpunished.** The return surface rewards the
  irregular visitor with what accumulated, and never names the gap.
- **He built the app and does not hold its coined vocabulary** — confirmed
  live during the grill ("i don't really understand what the gate is").
  Chrome speaks plain language, everywhere, always.

One sentence to design against: **a tired person walks in, speaks for eight
minutes, and leaves mid-thought — and the product must make that a complete,
honored visit.**

## 2. What the evidence convicts

1. **The rhythm is scattered.** The natural loop — walk in, see what waits,
   sit, review, leave — is spread across 15 surfaces behind 6 nav words, with
   home split in half and no way back into a live sitting.
2. **Half the product needed doors, not licenses.** Offer-only whispers plus
   gates tuned against imagined usage left a product where a real user
   experiences five things and never learns the other half exists.
3. **Silence is the dominant failure mode.** A parse failure that eats a
   sitting is indistinguishable from health.
4. **The grammar is duplicated and drifting.** Two review implementations,
   two gate rows, three parked objects in one row shape, five offer panels
   with colliding verbs.

## 4. The information architecture

Four places and a room. The shell shows four words; the room appears only
while a sitting is open.

```
today  ·  review  ·  about you  ·  your words        [ a sitting is open → ]
```

| Place | Absorbs | Dominant grammar |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | `mode` + `waiting` + activity | the dated page, one section per viewport |
| **The room** | `exchange` + `drm` + `unprompted` + soundings | the void |
| **Review** | `reviews` + `harvest` + `import-review` + `done` + buds | prose with cuts, focus-dimmed verdicts |
| **About you** | `wiki` + territory + correcting | the essay, behind the since-you-last-read lens |
| **Your words** | `material` + `piece` + `coach` | the board; tabs `passages · pieces · directions` |

What dies: `done`, `waiting`, `mode`, `import` as a nav word, all aliases —
one canonical name, one URL each. The import review gets a real route.
`/v2` stays untouched; note for the improvement loop: its personas measure
the old shell shape until someone re-points it.

## 5. The journey, surface by surface

### 5.1 First launch — no lobby

Setup (password, name/pronouns) flows **straight into the room**. One dimmed
promise line above the first question, gone after the first sitting:

> It keeps only your words — and you review everything it keeps.

Today does not exist until the first sitting has earned it. The three-line
contract (the person / the machine / the vault) lives findably in Today's
margin, not in the doorway.

### 5.2 Today — begin-first, one section per viewport

A dated page in sentences. Each section owns a viewport of the scroll —
arriving shows one clean thing, scrolling reveals the next. Order:

1. **Begin** (viewport one). "**begin**" — one word, one tap; no form, no
   ceremony, no declarations. Below it, dimmed, the three doors that pass
   the door test (§10): "walk back through yesterday · just write · shuffle
   a deck". Nothing else. Minutes and energy no longer exist as decisions
   (no discernible difference — the mode rewiring in §9 wave 1 removes
   their machinery too); a topic, if there is one, is spoken in the room.
   The cadence sentence sits under the date ("Last sitting 3 days ago, 4 in
   the last month.") — a long absence reads exactly like a short one.
   **When a sitting is open, the door replaces begin**: "A sitting is open —
   you left mid-thought · return / close it." Two open sittings is a state
   the product does not have. *Close it* is silent and mechanical: the
   closing section is written, the count shown, no questions asked.
2. **What waits** (viewport two). Reviews first ("Two sittings wait for your
   review."), then open questions (answer/park, answerable inline), then
   errands in the world, then parked things — **typed, three sentences, not
   one row shape**: "a thread going deeper, 4 steps kept · pick it up", "a
   day-walk, paused at noon · pick it up", "a question you set aside · take
   it back". An active import is just another sentence here ("nine pieces
   still wait from your import"). All offers (reach, coach, anniversary,
   backlog) render through one three-state grammar: offer / genuinely
   nothing / muted error — a check that errors says so quietly.
3. **Activity** (viewport three). Last eight lines, "more" expands. Zeros
   render as words.

Margin, dimmed: bring in past writing · start fresh… · stop the background
work · the three contracts.

### 5.3 The room — voice first, the true void

One room; the protocol changes the furniture, never the walls. One gate
implementation, one close, one abandonment behavior.

**First moments.** One dimmed line floats above the first question —
"about yourself — or name what this sitting is about" — and fades on your
first words. Touch it, or just say it ("about sourdough"): declaration by
utterance, in a voice-first product, at the moment it is true. Begin itself
never asks. There is no clock — minutes are not declared, and the app owns
the close.

**Speaking.** Hold-to-talk: hold the mic (or hold space), speak, release.
Bursts **append** across holds into the editable answer field with streaming
text as you speak — errors are visible at the moment you can still fix them
by voice. Send is explicit; a pause to think is never a committed turn.
Typing is the quiet fallback, always available.

**The void.** While answering: the question and your words, nothing else.
The transcript is summoned (a scroll, a glance gesture), not resident. No
clock, no chat bubbles, no feedback until done.

**Going deeper** (the descent). The app may offer to stay on one thread —
each question digging into the previous answer, up to 8–12 steps, entered
only by consent. During a descent the room shows its exit on every step,
three quiet words, resident not summoned:

> keep going · hold my place · not today

Three words, three outcomes: continue; park with the thread kept; close the
door with the record kept. The exit must not need summoning exactly when
the questions are deepest.

**Repair.** "not mine" on any quoted question, expanding once to
`unlink · keep`, rendered in the margin where the quote appears.

**Ending.** The person's own verb always works ("that's enough for today").
When the budget — a fixed question count, now that minutes are not
declared — empties, the app does not ask permission to close — it begins
the close: the open door ("anything else we didn't touch?"), the
bookmark ("where should we pick up?"), then the touchable count:

> Kept 4 passages for your review, 2 fragments couldn't stand alone —
> **read them now?**

Nothing verbatim shows at close. Warm review is one tap; it is never
automatic. Abandonment gets the same dignity deferred through Today's door.

### 5.4 Review — one grammar, focus-dimmed verdicts

Harvest review and import review are one surface, one implementation. The
source renders **whole, in order, as continuous prose with proposed cuts
underlined in place**. Touching a cut *focuses* it — the page dims around
it, and the verdict words appear in the margin: `keep · trim · say it
again · leave out` (live sittings; imports drop *say it again*, as ruled).
Trim is select-your-span on the prose — a gesture both thumbs and mice do
well. No per-row button sets, anywhere.

- Progress is a sentence ("3 of 7 decided"), finish-later keeps drafts.
- Fragments that couldn't stand alone appear at the end with their recorded
  reasons, so the coming question makes sense when it arrives.
- **The receipt is Review's last screen**: "Kept, in your words:" — the
  verbatim cuts the person just approved, and only those. Always
  post-consent by construction.
- A failed harvest is a sentence, not a silence: "Tuesday's sitting could
  not be read — its words are safe in the transcript · try again."

### 5.5 About you — the essay, behind the lens

The reading grammar survives untouched: two inks, ranges as em-dash
clauses, cites as dated quotes, contradictions as facing exhibits, status
carried typographically, correcting mode as the diff, six margin verbs on
focus plus "make this a direction".

What changes:

- **The since-you-last-read lens, on by default** whenever there are
  changes. Full ink: new claims, claims whose body or range was edited,
  claims whose status moved, contradiction exhibits opened or resolved,
  repair-touched claims. Everything else recedes. The whole essay is one
  word away.
- **The freshness line**, always at the top: "Read through Tuesday's
  sitting." — and when behind: "two sittings behind · let it catch up".
- "Touched by a repair — review" surfaces here, in the essay's idiom.

### 5.6 Your words — the board

Tabs: `passages · pieces · directions`. Passages and pieces are the library
kept as-is. Directions is a plain list of coached directions by name —
doors only, no aggregate view, no stats. The piece board keeps its grammar
(drag handles, gaps as quiet slots backed by questions, candidate
arrangements side by side, set down / pick up). A direction page keeps its
log-half and margin-advice-half shape.

## 6. The copy system

**Every string in the interface is redesigned.** The voice, pinned in the
grill: *plain companion* — with two riders: better terms where the current
plain ones still confuse, and **more explanatory copy wherever confusion is
possible**. Quiet does not mean unexplained.

Five rules, binding for every string:

1. **Plain words.** No coined term reaches the screen.
2. **Sentences, not labels.** The interface says things; it does not caption
   them.
3. **State facts, never gaps.** "Last sitting 3 days ago", never "it has
   been 3 days".
4. **No verdicts on the person.** The product describes its own process,
   never the person's adequacy.
5. **Every zero and every failure is a sentence.** "Rejected none." "The
   reach check did not run."

Flair is a failure mode: replacements are plain, not poetic.

| Internal name | On screen |
|---|---|
| harvest | what it kept / your words, kept |
| snippet | passage |
| sounding | going deeper |
| the gate | keep going · hold my place · not today |
| bud | a fragment that couldn't stand alone |
| DRM | walk back through yesterday |
| expedition | an errand in the world |
| marginalia | notes in the margin |
| wiki | about you |
| piece | piece (already plain) |
| sitting | sitting (already plain) |

Internal names stay in code, vault YAML, and docs — the schema is stable;
the screen never needs it.

## 7. The UI system

**Tokens.** The twelve `:root` tokens survive; add a dark theme
(`prefers-color-scheme`) — a product dictated to at night has no business
being paper-white only.

**Components** where today there are 263 free classes: `gate-row` (one
implementation), `review-cut` (underline-in-prose + focus-dim verdicts),
`offer-line` (the mandatory three-state panel), `parked-line` (typed),
`answer-field` (streaming dictation + append).

**Typography carries hierarchy; ink carries status; color stays reserved.**
Buttons remain words in running text except send and save.

**Phone pass with teeth.** Touch targets ≥ 44px including gate words and
margin verbs; the mic reachable by thumb; hold-to-talk without a keyboard;
the mode sentence cycles on tap.

**Known defects folded into the rebuild**: the coach page painting over the
waiting surface (missing `clear()`), `import` leaking the wiki observers
and the session clock interval, live-refresh rebuilding `unprompted`'s
textarea mid-write, route-contract test extended to the new route set.

## 8. What this does not fix, said plainly

The **ear** — register collapse, therapy lexicon, template monoculture,
picking trivia over freight — is elicitor quality, parked as fog, untouched
by any shell. The wiki's **invention problems** are clerk quality. This
redesign makes both *visible* (freshness line, receipt, streaming
dictation, failure sentences) and *repairable* (`not mine`, the six verbs);
the improvement loop owns the rest.

## 9. Implementation shape (for a later plan)

Phased so each wave lands whole, gates green between:

1. **Shell + Today** — four words, viewport sections, begin-first with the
   open-sitting door, the door set (begin · walk back through yesterday ·
   just write · shuffle a deck), offers unified, `done`/aliases/`waiting`/
   `mode` deleted — and the mode rewiring: budget becomes a constant, the
   descent license drops to three facts, `modeNeeds` and `sharpness` are
   deleted from queue entries and the draw (queue serialization
   conformance test updated with them).
2. **The close** — app-initiated close at budget, the count, silent close
   from Today, fragments surfaced.
3. **Review unification** — one grammar, prose-with-cuts, focus-dim
   verdicts, the receipt screen, failure sentences, routes for the
   routeless.
4. **The room** — instruments merged, true void with summoned chrome,
   descent-resident gate, hold-to-talk with streaming append (the one new
   server contract), first-launch flow with the promise line.
5. **About you + Your words** — the lens, freshness line, repair-touched
   entries, tabs, direction doors.
6. **The copy pass + dark theme + phone pass** — every string against the

Waves 1–3 and 5 are client-shaped except wave 1's mode rewiring (queue
fields, license, budget). Wave 4 carries the streaming-dictation contract.
The probation trials of §10 ride the improvement loop, not these waves.

## 10. The minimal product — the citation razor applied whole

Ruling (2026-08-09): every subsystem gets one of three verdicts. **Core**
has citations in the archive. **Probation** has none yet — it gets a paired
trial against a named floor under the improvement loop (the loop is the
executioner, not a tuner: if the floor wins or ties, the mechanism is
deleted, not re-tuned). **Cut** is convicted by the record.

### The door test (frontend)

A door onto a sitting must pass three rungs: **discernible** (the person
could tell this sitting from an ordinary one), **an arrival intent** (an
errand someone shows up with, in their own words — a technique is not an
errand), **doorway-bound** (it cannot be offered mid-sitting instead).
Doors that pass: **begin · walk back through yesterday · just write ·
shuffle a deck**. "Which two are alike" fails the intent rung: the triad
becomes a drawn question in the engine's repertoire, chip presentation
intact, its door deleted.

### Probation — mechanism · floor · the fingerprint that saves it

- **Claim minting (the clerk)** vs the contextualizer wiki (§11) — deep
  probation, leaning cut. The recorded claim layer produced negation drops,
  invented biography, bad merges, and zero ratified claims, while every
  star moment was snippet-plane. Saved only by beating §11 in a paired
  trial on the wiki-truer axis.
- **The coach** — held for data by ruling (not enough exists to convict or
  acquit). Floor: no coach. Its trial waits behind the wiki question — a
  coach needs something trustworthy to coach from. Until then the "make
  this a direction" verb keeps writing records; the machinery neither
  grows nor dies.
- **Expeditions** — held for data by the same ruling. Floor: none. The
  fingerprint that saves them: an errand-question producing a reflection
  the ordinary queue would not have reached.
- **Soundings** — zero offers ever fired, but the gates were re-derived
  from 957 replayed windows and the UX is ratified; trial runs at the new
  thresholds. Floor: no descent.
- **Contradiction candidate/re-measure machinery** vs raw juxtaposition
  ("same thing?"). The exhibit rendering is core either way.
- **Embedding resonance channel** vs lexical alone — with §12's fixes
  first; a starved channel cannot be fairly tried.

## 11. The wiki, rethought — the agent as contextualizer

The open question, stated plainly: **are the claims doing anything worthy?**
The recorded answer so far is no — and the razor's floor turns out to be a
better product than the mechanism it was the floor for. So the floor
becomes the default shape, and claims must beat it to return:

**The wiki is your words, organized. The agent's role is not to model you —
it is to hand each passage back with the context in which it was uttered.**

- The page is your passages, grouped into themes (neighborhoods, §12),
  in your ink.
- Under each, a context line in agent ink, marginalia-class, never
  quotable: *when* it was said, *what question* drew it, *what stood
  before it* in the conversation, *what it echoes or clashes with* across
  the years. Summarization scoped to the utterance and its circumstances —
  never a trait sentence about you.
- Contradiction exhibits stay: two of your quotes, dated, facing. The
  finding is the juxtaposition; no claim object required.
- The lens and the freshness line survive unchanged (new passages, new
  context lines, new exhibits; readings-behind counts).

Why this dissolves the recorded harm class: a context line cites lineage
facts that are mostly mechanical (date, eliciting question, position,
resonance hits) — there is no negation to drop, no modality to flatten, no
referent to fabricate, because the line describes the *utterance*, not the
*person*. The looping-effect risk drops with it: reading "said in March,
after a question about your father" changes you less than reading "you are
a person who…".

If the claim trial is ever run and won, claims return with their whole
apparatus (status, ranges, six verbs). Until then the six verbs shrink to
what the contextualizer needs: fix the context line's facts, unlink an
echo that is not one, and "make this a direction".

## 12. Embeddings, used like they matter

Recorded state: one channel, staged behind lexical, starved — 28 of 34
claims covered, 3 clash judgments per run against ~350 pending pairs,
duplicate snippets sailing through intake. The ruling: embeddings are
under-used and badly used. Where they earn their keep in the minimal
product:

1. **Intake dedupe** — 17–19 duplicate snippet bodies from one pasted
   answer is a solved problem; near-duplicate detection at harvest intake,
   surfaced as a review sentence ("this repeats what you said Tuesday —
   keep both?"), never a silent drop.
2. **Meaning-level resonance** — the idiolect argument that justified the
   channel (Q-17): the clash between disjoint vocabularies is the one
   thing lexical echo structurally cannot find. This is the embedding
   channel's trial ground.
3. **Neighborhoods** — the contextualizer wiki's grouping (§11): clustering
   your passages into themes is curation, not assertion, and it is exactly
   what embeddings do well locally. No question minting from clusters, no
   argmax; the grouping is a reading aid.
4. **Aboutness pooling** for exhibits — retrieval only; polarity stays
   judged elsewhere (Q-52's split holds).

Constraints kept: draws stay shuffle (Q-18), selection stays
constraints-then-chance (Q-13), the index stays derived and rebuildable
(Q-3). Debts owed before any trial: full-corpus coverage as a docket job,
quotas sized to the real corpus, and every embedding job logging its
coverage — starvation must be a sentence on the activity log, never a
silence.
