| Q-54 | Context-dependence is a RANGE REFINEMENT, not a third Contradiction type, and Q-21 already built the vocabulary for it: Range is mandatory on every Claim precisely so a belief cannot be stated context-free. Two claims whose bodies oppose but whose Ranges do not are not in contradiction, they are correctly scoped; the un-drawn boundary is the case where both Ranges are the same or both vague. So the answer to "where does the first hold, where does the second" is a narrowed Range on EACH pole — `SUPERSEDE` with reason, a verb Q-29 already has. No third Contradiction type, no new op, no new Protocol instrument. Mechanism: a fifth `ClashCandidate` outcome `range-discriminated`, whose consequence is one SUPERSEDE per pole with reason `range-discriminated:<candidateId>`, citing both poles' snippets plus the discriminating answer's. Because Q-53 forces that answer into a different sitting from both poles, Q-50 makes the refined claims born `evidenced` rather than `unconfirmed` — the most valuable claims in the wiki arrive with the strongest status mechanically, without anyone deciding it should be so. The discriminating question comes AFTER the re-measure and only on the dissolution branch, never instead of it: asking "when does X hold versus Y" before opposition is established presupposes both poles and leads the witness, and Q-30 stage 2's "exactly one question" is a rule about the gate on opening a Contradiction, which a follow-up on an already-dissolved candidate is not. Often no second question is needed at all, because the dissolving answer named the condition itself. TWO DOORS, and the second ships first: the pipeline door above inherits every recall problem in Q-52, and hanging the highest-value output in the system off its flakiest machinery is not acceptable — so a zero-LLM lint finding under Q-31 (two live claims sharing a registry referent whose Ranges are identical or near-identical) mints discriminating questions independently, with no candidate pool and no opposition detection in the path. It works on the corpus that exists today. | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02); HANDOFF review finding |
| Q-53 | A re-measure counts only from a DIFFERENT SITTING, and the predicate is the session boundary rather than a wall clock. The confirming reading's `Provenance.session` must differ from the session of BOTH claims in the pair — not merely be later than `remeasureAskedAt`, which only records when the question was minted. Computed mechanically from the same field and the same cite→session resolution Q-50 uses; one rule, two uses. Rationale: the failure Q-14 exists to prevent is LABILITY UNDER QUESTIONING, and lability is a property of a continuous conversational frame — the momentum of the last half hour, the vocabulary the elicitor introduced, the self the person has been performing. A session boundary ends that frame; elapsed time does not track it. A 24-hour minimum is wrong in both directions: two short sittings twenty minutes apart on unrelated topics are separate measurements, and one six-hour sitting is not. Session is not a proxy for time here, it is the variable. Corollary on expiry: `remeasure-expired` is the ONE outcome that does not retire the pair, because expiry is a question that fell off the queue, not an answer — silence must never stand in for a verdict. A candidate carries an `attempts` counter, earns exactly one re-proposal after an expiry, and retires on the second; every other outcome retires the pair at once (B9 anti-repetition otherwise intact). | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02); HANDOFF review finding |
| Q-52 | The clash channels retrieve ABOUTNESS; polarity is judged one layer down and nowhere else. Lexical (shared phrase), referent (shared registry entity) and embedding (cosine) all answer "are these two claims about the same thing" — none can see negation, and none is asked to. `judgeOpposition` is the polarity organ, and it is anchored: `poleA`/`poleB` must be exact substrings of the cited quotes, code-verified, or the candidate drops. A channel that COULD see negation would separate the two poles of a contradiction and never pool them — negation-blindness is what makes an opposed pair a near-neighbour, so the property the 2026-08-02 HANDOFF review read as the blind spot is the retrieval behaviour the pipeline depends on. What the review is right about is not recall but PRECISION: an aboutness-only pool spends a bounded judgment quota on pairs that mostly agree, and `WikiReport` today cannot tell "the pool was empty" from "the pool was full of agreement" from "no contradictions exist" — the observability failure of eval finding #8 repeating. So no fourth NLI channel is added on argument: the pool is instrumented first (size, per-channel contribution, opposed ratio), and a cross-encoder graduates on that record under Q-35 or not at all. Adding a mechanism to fix an unobserved failure is the thing Q-35 exists to prevent. | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02); HANDOFF review finding |
| Q-51 | Material whose authorship cannot be SEPARATED is not admissible corpus, and the test is structural rather than a judgement about quality. Co-authored prose — a jointly-run talk, a co-written piece, notes attributed to two people — is excluded whole, because no cut of it can be verified as the user's own sentence and Sole Authorship (Q-1) guarantees wording rather than origin. Ruled 2026-08-02 over the 2017-2026 published-writing ingest: the co-authored talks stay out. The rule extends past that ingest — anything entering the vault by a path other than elicitation must be separable, and where it is not, the whole item is excluded rather than sampled carefully. Quoted and cited passages inside a single-authored work are the same problem at a smaller scale and are excluded at cut level, not item level. Exclusion is reversible and admission is not: a wrongly-excluded item can be ingested later, while a wrongly-admitted sentence becomes a Claim about a person who never held it. | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02) |
| Q-50 | Two cites are INDEPENDENT only when they come from different SITTINGS. Cross-sitting is the floor and promotes `unconfirmed` → `evidenced`; cites additionally separated by Facet or by question source are the stronger tier and are recorded as such, so a later decision can use the distinction without re-deriving it. Rationale: two cuts from one answer on one thread are one thought said twice, and if that promotes a claim then every claim reaches `evidenced` within a month and the Status vocabulary stops carrying information — the specific way epistemic metadata usually dies. What `evidenced` is meant to mean is that a claim survived being approached again on a different day. The predicate is mechanical and computed from Provenance.session, never model-judged (Q-29). Must be in force before the Clerk's first run: `evidenced` cannot be retrofitted across a claim graph. | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02); HANDOFF review finding |
| Q-49 | The contradiction pipeline's stage-1 opposition judgment ships LIVE, not shadow — the one deliberate exception to Q-35's shadow-first rule, decided with the argument recorded. Q-30 defines a candidate AS two claims with model-judged opposed Stances: the judgment is what makes a candidate exist, not a gate applied to one, so shadowing it means no candidate ever exists and Q-30 stages 2-5 are dead code. What Q-35 protects against is a mechanism ACTING on an unproven judgment; here the only consequence is one guarded, non-accusatory re-measure question the user may ignore, and the irreversible step — opening a Contradiction — stays gated by stage 3's code checks over a verbatim quote (Q-44/Q-46 satisfied). Every other threshold in the slice remains shadow-first. | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02) |
| Q-48 | Two models by ROLE, not one model for everything. The ELICITOR (probes, red-lights, live composition — a human is waiting) uses the fast model; the CLERK (harvest, wiki jobs, consolidation, minting — background work, Q-22 means nobody waits) uses the careful model. Measured basis: bonsai-27b answers in 3-9s but collapsed on long structured payloads and produced echo-degenerate probes; qwen3.6:35b returns clean JSON first try but is far slower per call. Latency is a UX constraint only in the foreground; instruction-following is a correctness constraint only in the background. Both stay local (ADR-0001). Q-34 model stamps must record WHICH model produced each artifact, so the two roles remain separable in the record. Either role can be pointed anywhere by env var; the split is a default, not a coupling. | Session decision (Micah, 2026-08-02) |
| Q-47 | A Sounding BECOMES the rest of the sitting: entering converts the remaining question budget into the descent's rung allowance (capped 8-12 per CONTEXT), and the two close moves (Q-20) are reserved BEYOND it, always. Rungs neither silently consume the close nor run unbounded — an uncapped consent-gated descent is how consent becomes an endurance test, however good the gates are. Because the total is knowable in advance, the user is told roughly how long the descent runs when asked to consent (Q-43), which is what makes the consent meaningful. A sitting that went deep is the one whose bookmark matters most, so the close is never sacrificed. | Session decision (Soundings grill Q5) |
| Q-46 | A Sounding ends structurally, never by model self-report: at the rung cap (8-12, mechanical), OR at convergence detected in CODE — the last two rungs produce answers that echo prior rungs (the same lexical machinery Resonance uses) or an answer is content-free by the pivot heuristic already in the elicitor. The agent is never asked "have we converged?" Same principle as Q-44 and adversarial-eval finding #6: no model self-report where a structural check will do. Ending is always announced as the descent closing, never as the user failing to go deeper. | Session decision (Soundings grill Q4) |
| Q-45 | A parked Sounding preserves THE LADDER: the ordered rungs (each question with the answer it drew), where the descent stopped, and which gate choice ended it — not a bare rung number (useless without the chain) and not a pre-composed next question (authored for a person who no longer exists by the time they return; compose fresh at resume, when what happened since is also visible). RESUMING IS COMPACTED, because the local model degrades on long payloads (adversarial eval finding #1): the prompt carries the last 1-2 rungs verbatim plus a one-line summary of the earlier ones, reusing the Cover consolidation mechanism (ADR-0002 layer 3). The full ladder stays on disk as truth (Q-3); the compaction is a prompt-time view. Ladder summaries are agent prose — Marginalia-class, never a Snippet, never shown at close (Q-20). | Session decision (Soundings grill Q3) |
| Q-44 | The mid-descent consent gate is ALWAYS PRESENT, never triggered: every rung carries the same three quiet margin words (continue / park, depth kept / another day), plus a mechanical checkpoint at the halfway rung. Discomfort is NEVER model-inferred — a model self-reporting a boolean is a suggestion, not a check (adversarial eval finding #6), and applying that to a person's emotional state is presumptuous as well as unreliable: an agent that says "I sense this is hard" on pattern-matched short sentences has told the user something false about themselves, the looping-effect harm the design guards everywhere else. An always-available gate is stronger than detection because stopping never requires being noticed; the counter-based checkpoint breaks answering-momentum without diagnosing anyone. Recorded signal is behavioral only — where you parked, how long you took, whether you chose another day. | Session decision (Soundings grill Q2) |
| Q-43 | A Sounding is agent-proposed and user-consented, never auto-entered. The PROPOSAL itself is licensed: late in the sitting, a Mode with energy for it, and a thread that has already sustained 3+ turns on one construct. Declining costs one word, is recorded as signal, and is never re-offered in the same sitting — a second ask converts an offer into pressure. Consent to ENTER is not consent to CONTINUE (the mid-descent gate is separate). Rationale: auto-entry is the interrogation Q-15 forbids; user-initiation alone would require the person mid-thought to self-diagnose that a thread has become load-bearing, which is the burden the tool exists to carry. | Session decision (Soundings grill Q1) |
| Q-42 | The composition slice ships in two passes, both in scope, in this order. Pass 1 is ZERO-LLM and complete on its own: manual initiation, one deterministic chronological Arrangement, reorder / remove / write-new-prose (Q-40) / insert-Gap (Q-39), export to markdown with pinned versions inlined, manual set-down (Q-41). Pass 2 adds the model: candidate Arrangements under distinct organizing principles (Q-38), skeleton Marginalia, stale-pin lint, auto-set-down. Agent OFFERS (Q-37) are excluded from both — they are licensed by citation-cluster density, which requires Claims, so they land with or after the Clerk slice. Rationale: arranging your own sentences is the product thesis and needs no model; a deterministic reviewer cannot hallucinate, cannot stall on a slow call, and makes pass 2 a pure addition rather than a correction. | Session decision (composition grill Q6) |
| Q-41 | A Piece is never "finished" — it is SET DOWN, reversibly. Setting down (by the user, or silently after long dormancy) stops Gap-question minting and lets existing gap questions expire on the normal queue rule; the Arrangement stays fully editable and picking it up resumes minting. No done flag, because "done" manufactures its complement — everything else becomes unfinished, which is the shame gradient Q-24 forbids (dormancy is signal, never debt). A set-down Piece stays first-class: its pins still count as citations, its Snippets still resonate, it still appears in re-reading. Auto-set-down is silent and logged (Q-23), never announced (Q-22). | Session decision (composition grill Q5) |
| Q-40 | Prose the user writes inside a Piece (transitions, connecting clauses, titles) becomes a Snippet — harvested like any other user prose, with composition provenance.
