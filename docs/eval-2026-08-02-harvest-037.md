# Harvest eval — ticket 037, against 295 hand-marked cuts

Ticket 037 says the harvester drops episodes and mislabels facet and stance.
`docs/ingest-triage-2026-08-02.md` is the first labelled set large enough to
check that: 295 real proposals over nine years of Micah's published writing,
each marked keep or drop with a reason code, produced by the live clerk model
on the real harvest path. This file records what the harvester did against that
set before ticket 037, what changed, and what did not.

Two measurements are reported and they are not the same kind of thing.

**Offline** replays the frozen 295 cuts through the gates. It costs nothing,
repeats exactly, and answers every question about admissibility.

**Live** is a 19-turn sample of the same corpus, run twice against
`qwen3.6:35b` — once with the old system prompt, once with the new one. It is
the only way to see facet and stance at all, because the review file recorded
cut text and nothing else. 19 of the 59 corpus turns, chosen to cover the
capstone's field notes, the burnout passage, the two quotation-heavy turns, and
five whole small posts.

## The set, and one correction to it

The triage doc's own arithmetic is off by one in two places, and the code is

- Capstone cut **86** ("It was heartwarming to see that they were so excited to

Neither changes a conclusion. Both are recorded so the next person to load the

## Before: the 044 gate is inert on prose

`src/harvester/admissibility.ts` rejected **0 of 295** cuts. Not 0 of the hard
ones — 0 of all of them, including all 9 the triage marked `frag`, which is the
exact class the ticket predicted it should have caught.

That is not a bug in the gate. The gate was built for conversation, against
"Yes.", "dunno" and "This question makes no sense." Nothing in nine years of
published prose looks like that. The gate is inert here because prose does not
contain the failure it was written to stop.

## The gate that is not here

The criterion Micah used is one sentence: *would this be evidence about the
person if you did not know who wrote it?* Six candidate mechanizations were
measured against all 295 cuts:

| predicate | catches of 114 `world`+`log` | keeps destroyed | precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| no first-person singular | 76 | 53 | 48% |
| no first-person and no stance verb | 32 | 16 | 54% |
| past-tense action verb, no stance verb | 37 | 14 | 69% |
| third-person and past-tense action | 20 | 4 | 74% |

Calling every cut junk already scores 53%, because 53% of them are. The best
rule buys 21 points of precision and pays in Micah's own sentences.

The rules are not crude; the distinction is not structural. "The workshop space
is where five brothers work from" (drop) and "Fragility is an honest and
important understanding because it changes the way we look at incentives"
(keep) are both third-person declaratives. "I made a rough spreadsheet which I
shared with Padmini" (drop) and "I started to play with the idea of researching
through doing" (keep) are both first-person past. One reports an action, the
other reports a stance. No regex sees that, because the difference is what the
sentence is *about*.

**So this one is not mechanizable, and nothing was shipped for it.** The
judgement stays with the person, in review. It also must not be handed to a
model call: that is the self-report the admissibility gate exists to route
around, and it would be a gate nobody had evaluated. The absence is recorded in
`src/harvester/admissibility.ts` with the numbers, so the next person does not
re-derive it from first principles.

## What was shipped, and what it measures

### 1. Q-51 at cut level, inside the harvester

`scripts/ingest-posts.ts` already had `isQuotedFromSource`, and it worked — but
only in the importer, and only after the review file had already been written
with seven of Annemarie Mol's and Sara Ahmed's sentences in it as Micah's
prose. The same predicate, applied to the *turn* the harvester was handed
rather than to the source file:

**7 of 7 quoted cuts caught. 0 false positives on the other 288.**

It is now `admissible(text, { source: turn.text })`. Every harvest path gets it,
not just the importer. Q-1 is untouched: the rule only ever subtracts, and the
exact-substring check still stands on its own.

### 2. A structural standalone gate — mid-sentence cuts become Buds

Eval finding #6 says `standalone` is the model grading its own homework, and
ticket 035 closed without the structural check it promised. Two candidate
checks, measured on the same 295:

| check | catches of 9 `frag` | keeps caught |
|---|---|---|
| leading bare pronoun or demonstrative (035's proposal) | **0** | **25** |
| starts on a lowercase letter (shipped) | 7 | 4 |

035's rule is a keep shredder. Real prose opens sentences with expletive "It
was interesting to see…" and discourse "This modularity would allow…"
constantly; all 25 hits are Micah's keeps and none is a fragment. **Do not
implement it as written.**

The lowercase test ships, and it routes to the **Bud** path rather than
dropping. A Bud keeps the fragment and can be asked about, so its four false
positives cost a delay, not a loss. The two `frag` cuts it misses are full
grammatical sentences with dangling referents ("…I came across the biggest
issue." — which issue?). No structural test reaches those.

### 3. Facet and stance read off the text where the text says so

- **`superseded`** never fired in either eval, including on "I used to think X.
  I no longer think that." The markers now force the stance whatever the model
  said. `used to` requires a mental verb behind it, so "I used to work at the
  shop" stays habitual past.
- **`intention`** was applied to 5 of ~14 cuts and correct on none — the
  model's don't-know bucket. A cut labelled `intention` with no want, plan or
  goal in the person's own words is now **counted and left alone**. The marker
  proves the label is wrong and says nothing about which of the seven other
  facets is right, and a wrong label swapped for a different wrong label is not
  a fix. The prompt is the fix; the counter is how anyone knows whether it
  worked.
- **`episode`** cannot be corrected after the fact, because the failure is a cut
  that was never proposed. `episodeAnchoredTurns` and `episodeBlindTurns` count
  turns that name when something happened and yielded no episode cut. Shadow
  measurement, per Q-35.

### 4. The prompt

Facet and stance were bare enum values. Each now carries the test that
separates it from its neighbours, `episode` is named as the priority when a
turn holds both an occasion and the belief drawn from it, the supersession
markers are spelled out, and cuts are told to start and stop on sentence
boundaries.
