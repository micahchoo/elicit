## 7. Hazards

1. Concurrent writers → freeze tree, record SHA, no watch mode.
2. Model-calling jobs: sweep 12×~29s; candidates ≤3 judgments; confirmation;
   embeddings under 300s budget / 120s per-request timeout. Long silence ≠ hang.
3. Midnight log rotation → always glob.
4. console.error carries drain failures the log does not → keep the tee.
5. Every job is guard()-wrapped: `wiki-jobs-failed` beside a low `applied`
   is a partial run, not a model failure — check before attributing.
6. `expire()` never touches a drawn entry: an abandoned re-measure stays
   `asked` forever and never reaches remeasuresExpired — check the queue
   before calling the mechanism broken.
7. Zero is a measurement: clash-checked fires on empty runs; report zeros.
8. Do not loosen Q-50; the unconfirmed wall is named in advance.
