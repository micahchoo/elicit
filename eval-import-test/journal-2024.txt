August 12, 2024

Started the morning with coffee on the porch. The maple out front is starting to turn early this year — a few red leaves already at the crown. I remember Dad saying that meant a cold winter. He was right about half the time.

Work was fine. The Henderson account is finally closing after six months of back-and-forth. I don't know why these things take so long when everyone knows what the answer is going to be. It's like we're all just waiting for permission to say what we already think.

Called Mom after dinner. She's doing better since the surgery but still gets tired by three in the afternoon. She mentioned wanting to visit in October — I said yes without thinking about it. Now I'm looking at my calendar and October is already full. I'll figure it out.
